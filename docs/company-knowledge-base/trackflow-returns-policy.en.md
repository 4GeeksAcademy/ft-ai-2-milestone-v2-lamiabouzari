# TrackFlow returns policy

## Domestic returns

The supplied TrackFlow knowledge-base material does not document a general domestic returns window, fee, eligibility rule, or automatic authorization process. Do not promise a return period or characterize a domestic return as automatically approved. Confirm the customer's applicable agreement and shipment details with the account team before advising on a specific return.

## International returns

International returns are not automatic. They require manual handling. A TrackFlow salesperson or account manager must not describe an international return as automatically approved, automatically generated, or automatically routed. Explain that the request needs manual handling and have the account team confirm the applicable next steps for the shipment.

## Refunds, fees, and exceptions

No refund timing, return fee, customs treatment, eligibility exception, or reimbursement amount is documented in the supplied material. These details must be checked against the applicable customer agreement and confirmed by the account team. If the approved information is unavailable, say so rather than inventing a policy.
