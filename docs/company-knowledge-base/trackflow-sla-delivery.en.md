# TrackFlow delivery service levels

## Standard delivery commitments

A specific standard delivery SLA, delivery-time range, service availability guarantee, or claim remedy is not documented in the knowledge base supplied for this implementation. Confirm the applicable service terms for the customer and shipment before communicating a delivery commitment. Do not infer a standard SLA from historical performance or another customer's service.

## Black Friday, Sales, and other high-demand dates

Black Friday, Sales periods, and other declared high-demand peaks can affect delivery operations. TrackFlow must never promise a delivery SLA during these declared high-demand dates. A salesperson or account manager may explain that delivery timing can be affected, but must not describe an estimated time as guaranteed. Confirm any shipment-specific update with the operations team; do not turn an operational estimate into a contractual commitment.

## Shipment-specific questions and exceptions

The documents supplied here do not define a shipment-specific escalation time, compensation, or exception process. If a customer asks for a precise commitment or remedy that is not stated in approved terms, say that the available information is insufficient and ask the account team to confirm it. Do not invent delivery windows, percentages, penalties, or exceptions.
