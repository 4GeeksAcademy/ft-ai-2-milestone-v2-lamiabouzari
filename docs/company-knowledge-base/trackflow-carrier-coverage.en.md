# TrackFlow carrier coverage

## Coverage information available

The supplied TrackFlow knowledge-base material does not contain an approved carrier-by-country, carrier-by-region, or carrier-by-postcode coverage list. Do not claim that a particular carrier serves a destination unless the current coverage has been verified for that shipment.

## Destination and service checks

For a customer's destination, carrier availability, service type, and any transit commitment must be confirmed with TrackFlow operations or the account team. Coverage can be shipment- and service-specific; do not infer coverage from a carrier name, a neighboring region, or an undocumented prior shipment.

## Carrier exceptions

An undocumented carrier exception, route, or service is not approved by this knowledge base. Treat it as requiring internal confirmation and approval before communicating it as available. If no verified coverage information is available, tell the customer that there is not enough confirmed information to answer yet.
