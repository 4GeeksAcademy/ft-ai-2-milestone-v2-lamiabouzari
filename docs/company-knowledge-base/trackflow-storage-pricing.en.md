# TrackFlow storage pricing and discounts

## Storage pricing

The supplied TrackFlow knowledge-base material does not document storage rates, billing units, minimums, surcharges, or a customer-specific price. Never quote or estimate a storage price from this document. Confirm the applicable written customer agreement or obtain an approved quote from the account team.

## Storage discounts and approval

A storage discount must not be offered or represented as approved without Miguel Torres's approval. Any request for a storage discount requires Miguel Torres's approval before it is communicated as an offer. Do not imply that approval is guaranteed or already granted.

## Undocumented pricing and exceptions

An undocumented storage discount, rate exception, free-storage period, or pricing concession requires approval and confirmation by the account team. When the approved price or approval status is unavailable, explain that there is not enough confirmed information to quote it. Never invent prices, percentages, savings, or discount terms.
